# Paalvi — Brand & Design Brief

**Paalvi — everyday essentials, elevated.**
Premium toothbrushes, toothpaste, oral care and daily essentials for Indian homes and businesses. The site should make a toothbrush feel like a design object, the way Aesop makes hand soap feel special.

## Mood
Aesop (calm, editorial) × Muji (honest simplicity) × Marvis (playful premium oral care) × Apple product pages (macro detail, clarity).

## Principles
1. **Clean = trust.** In personal care, visual cleanliness signals hygiene. Generous white space, soft light, no clutter, no shouting discount banners.
2. **The product is the hero.** Macro photography and video: bristle tips, paste texture, foam, droplets. Cards sit on soft tinted grounds so every pack reads like a still life.
3. **Honest ingredients.** Key ingredients, "free-from" badges and how-to-use are first-class content, never buried. The AI guide only repeats claims the owner has entered.
4. **Motion feels like water and air.** Buoyant springs (`float 90/18`, `gentle 120/20`, `snappy 400/30`), one easing curve `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`, nothing aggressive. Everything respects reduced-motion.
5. **Mobile is the flagship.** Designed at 390px first, then 768px and 1440px+. Effects drop away automatically on low-end Android phones (≤4 cores / ≤4 GB / Save-Data).
6. **WhatsApp-first conversion.** Every product, section and business segment ends in one tap to WhatsApp with a pre-filled, structured message. Each tap is also logged as a lead.

## Identity: "Paalvi Fresh" (the default theme; the owner can change it)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Porcelain | `#F8F7F4` | page surface |
| Cloud | `#FFFFFF` | cards, panels |
| Deep Ink | `#14181F` | text, primary buttons |
| Slate | `#5B6470` | muted text |
| Seafoam Mint | `#8FC9B9` | primary, highlights |
| Soft Clay | `#E6CFC3` | secondary tints |
| Brushed Champagne | `#BFA77A` | premium accents, used sparingly |

- **Type:** Instrument Serif for display (or Fraunces), used sparingly for headlines; Manrope for UI and body; Tiro Devanagari Hindi for festival themes.
- **Wordmark:** "PAALVI" in the display serif, tracking 0.2em.
- **Shapes:** pebble-soft radii (8 / 16 / 28 / pill); shadows diffused and tinted with the primary colour, never black.
- **Glass:** "frosted bathroom glass": 20px blur, 160% saturation, a translucent white fill and a faint noise grain. It falls back to solid on weak devices.
- **Icons:** thin 1.5px line icons (Lucide).

## Festivals
24 curated presets (12 everyday, 12 festival). Each one swaps colours, the display font and a gentle ambient layer: bubbles, mist, ripples, diyas, gulal, snow or petals. Festivals switch on by date; a manual override always wins. Every preset passes WCAG AA contrast.
